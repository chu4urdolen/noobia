# Rolling microphone rise detector — 2026-09-23

Firmware 0.4.7 implements a reusable ten-bucket rise detector around Iris's
native `MIC_LEVEL` function. It samples a small RMS chunk every 100 ms and
immediately accumulates that integer into a one-second bucket. The only history
is ten `int32_t` bucket means (40 bytes); no raw audio is retained.

The rolling comparison is updated once per second:

    newer_five_mean >= older_five_mean * 200%
    newer_five_mean >= 20 RMS

After an event it rearms after three consecutive bucket updates for which the
condition is false. BLE and VM controls use native IDs 212 through 216.

## Live verification

Twelve direct idle readings ranged from RMS 4 to 15. The rolling quiet window
settled at `older=6 newer=6`. During the sustained-noise test, the means moved
through louder buckets and finally reached `older=6 newer=25`. At that point:

- the detector emitted and disarmed;
- the VM consumed the timestamp, leaving the detector queue empty;
- `POLICE_SEQUENCE` ran both channels to 11/11;
- the VM remained waiting for the next event;
- the detector continued running and later rearmed.

`police_on_loudness_rise.hex` loaded 54 bytes and was saved on Iris's SD card
as `/programs/police_on_loudness_rise.nvm`.

The verified build used 1,397,178 bytes of flash and 64,248 bytes of global RAM.
